What Are Language Barriers?

Language barriers happen when people who speak completely different languages try to chat but just can't understand each other properly. This causes massive stress for international teenagers moving to a new high school here in New Zealand. Trying to constantly translate English phrases in your head all day long gives you huge brain strain and translation fatigue, which makes lots of new students feel totally left out and isolated in the classroom. This hub is built to give students quick, easy access to simple help guides so they never have to feel stuck or lonely at school.

What Can You Do?

Dealing with language barriers and translation fatigue at high school can feel really tough, but there are actually heaps of easy ways to get help at Green Bay High School. For starters, you can just download quick translation apps on your phone or add extensions to your laptop. It makes reading long, heavy texts in class way faster so your brain doesn't get completely fried. Also, you can always drop by the international student support room during recess or lunch. The staff there are super nice, they have school guides in different languages, and they can even set you up with an older student to help with homework. Finally, going along to the lunch clubs and peer mentoring groups is a choice way to make new friends, practice speaking English without feeling stressed, and make sure you never feel left out or lonely at school.
